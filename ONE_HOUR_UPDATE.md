# One-Hour Delivery Update

This version tells users:
- The report is expected in less than 1 hour after submission.
- A success panel appears after submission.
- A 1-hour countdown starts.
- If the report is not received after 1 hour, the user is told to contact support.
- A direct support email link is included.

Upload to GitHub:
- index.html
- README.md
- .nojekyll

No Apps Script redeploy is needed if you only replace index.html.
